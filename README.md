

# Lasse — building **Layline**

> **Find your layline.** I build products that optimize for **Velocity Made Good** — real progress toward a destination, not motion for its own sake.

In sailing, a layline is the course from which you can reach the mark without another tack. Most worthwhile outcomes can't be reached in a straight line; progress comes from the right sequence of moves under real constraints. Layline is a **decision-systems portfolio** built on that idea — not an "AI wrapper," but products where deterministic systems own the facts and models supply judgment.

### How I build

Solo, with an AI development team kept in **deliberately separate roles** — strategy, building, and **independent adversarial review** — on one principle: *the builder is never the final reviewer.* Decisions are logged, changes are measured, and **nothing consequential happens silently.** Matching/quality changes are **eval-gated**: they have to prove they improved the result on a frozen test set before they ship.

### Engineering principles

- **Deterministic facts, AI judgment.** Systems own the facts; models interpret and explain.
- **Measure, don't assume.** Evaluations decide whether complexity earns its place.
- **Human agency.** Nothing consequential happens silently.
- **Simple wins.** Infrastructure is justified only when it improves the result.
